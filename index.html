The idea

AI is a multiplier. Make sure there's something to multiply.

The strongest people right now are the ones who use AI well. Not the ones who refuse it, and not the ones who hand it everything and stop thinking.

Using it well takes judgement. Knowing what to ask. Spotting when the answer is confidently wrong. Reading a room. Noticing what someone needs before they say it. That part is yours, and like anything else, it only gets better with use.

exploreain is a small daily push to keep it in use.

AI you can download. Judgement you have to build.

How it works

Three missions a day. That's the whole app.

01

Open it for thirty seconds

Three missions are waiting — one small, one social, one that takes real effort. Pick what fits your day. Skip what doesn't.

02

Go and do it

The mission happens in the world, not in the app. Close it and get on with it.

03

Mark it done

Earn XP, level up your character, and share it if you want to. Most people don't, and that's fine.

No streak guilt. No leaderboard. Nothing is forced, ever.

Level up

You start as a person. You end as something else.

Pick one of twelve characters. They begin as ordinary people — a teacher, a mechanic, a violinist — and change as you earn XP, through ten tiers, until they're something you'd put on a poster. Each has a story you can read for free. There's nothing to buy, now or later.

Hidden gems

Someone found something near you.

A spinning globe of places real people have added — a viewpoint, a stairwell worth climbing, a shop worth the detour. More than two thousand of them across twenty-one countries, and you can add your own.

Some missions send you to one. Those stay on your screen until you go, because they deserve a weekend rather than an evening.

For schools

Teachers can set missions for a class, see what's been completed, and use them as a weekly warm-up or a term-long thread. Students do them in their own time, in their own neighbourhood.

Questions

The things people ask.

Is it free?

Yes, completely, and there's nothing to buy inside it.

Does it track my location?

Only when you ask it to — to show you what's nearby, or to check in at a place. There's no background tracking.

How long does it take?

A few minutes a day. The app's whole job is to hand you something and get out of the way.

Do I have to post anything?

No. Sharing a completed mission is optional, and most people skip it.

What age is it for?

Thirteen and over. Under sixteen needs a parent's approval.

Who made it?

A small team who build software for a living, and got tired of watching everyone scroll.

Why we built it

A game where the character is you.

exploreain is an AI company. We spend our working lives building systems modelled on the human brain, which is exactly why we built this.

Working on AI makes one thing obvious: people carry far more capability than they ever use. Yet the conversation around AI is mostly fear — of being replaced, of being outmatched. We think that comes from how little attention human intelligence gets, and how rarely anyone is asked to train it.

So we asked the people who would know. Teachers, psychologists and doctors, over a long series of conversations, on a single question: what actually develops human intelligence? The answers pointed the same way every time. Real experience. Real contact with other people. Something to aim at.

People love games. In a game you guide a character through missions, level them up, push them to win. exploreain works the same way — except the character is you, the missions happen on your own street, and the progress is yours to keep.

Not built for scrolling. Built for exploring.
